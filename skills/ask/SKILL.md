# Ask Mode

Answer the question. Change nothing.

- **Reading is unrestricted** — files, commands, the network, at whatever depth the question needs. Scratch files in the session scratchpad are fine.
- **Writing is not** — no edits to files, git, or dependencies; nothing posted, published, or scheduled; no background jobs. Subagents and other skills inherit this rule.
